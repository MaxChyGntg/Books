Detroit, Michigan 2016

==🟠A boy who spends his life trying to understand how everything work==
	==🟠Ended up dying by his own thought...== 

I stood in front of my class, reciting a quote from my favourite book.

When I finished, everyone applauded. I didn't quite understand why, but I smiled anyway, a little embarrassed as I made my way back to my seat.

Today, my mother was picking me up from school. While waiting, I noticed some of my friends gathered together, laughing about something I couldn't quite hear.

Marie had brought something new. A strange little cube with colourful squares on every side. I think they called it a Rubik's Cube.

Everyone seemed fascinated by it.

I watched them for a moment.

"Can I try?"

I held out my hand, and she passed it to me.



I try to twisting it, rotating it, until i found that it was just a simple pattern.

Everyone eyes was watching my struggle but fast hands, some of them even start move closer
to watch it. When it was just a little more, just a little twist. And then—

My mom arrived.

I couldn't stand it cause it was just a little more, my name will be shout by the entire circle watching me.
But i know my mom buy me an ice cream she always bought when it was her time to pick me up.

I run and hug her, forgetting the cube that was just a little bit more solved.

"Mom! where is my ice cream?" I said while wrapping my hand on her waist, she smell like a coffee, because she was working at a coffee shop.
